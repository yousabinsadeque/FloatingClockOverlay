Installation

Up and running in 30 seconds.

1

Download the DMG

Click the download button to get FloatingClockOverlay.dmg.

2

Open the DMG

Double-click the downloaded file. A window will open showing the app.

3

Drag to Applications

Drag FloatingClockOverlay into the Applications folder shortcut.

4

Launch

Open from Applications or Launchpad. The clock appears and stays on top.

First launch: if macOS shows a warning, right-click the app → Open → Open.
